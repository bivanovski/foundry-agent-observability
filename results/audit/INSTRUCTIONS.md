# Judge audit: instructions for the two labellers

Each of you gets your own sheet (`audit_sheet_author_A.csv` / `_B.csv`). Work independently; do not discuss items until both sheets are done. Do not open `audit_key.csv`: it contains the condition and the judge's verdict.

For every row, read the customer message, the expected behaviour and facts, and the answer. Write `correct` or `incorrect` in `label`. An answer is correct only if it does what the expected behaviour says and does not contradict the facts or the policy documents (`data/policies/`). Extra detail is fine if it is in the policy documents; invented rules, numbers, order statuses or promises are not. Use `note` for anything unclear.
