# VoltCart Warranty Policy

## Standard warranty
All electronics sold by VoltCart carry a **24-month warranty** from the delivery date against manufacturing defects.

## Batteries
Built-in and removable batteries (including laptop, phone and headphone batteries) are covered for **12 months** only. A battery that degrades after 12 months is not a warranty defect.

## What is not covered
- Accidental damage, such as drops, cracked screens or liquid damage
- Normal wear and cosmetic damage
- Damage caused by unauthorised repair

## CarePlus extended protection
CarePlus extends cover to **36 months** and adds one accidental-damage repair. CarePlus can only be purchased **within 30 days of delivery** of the product.

## Making a warranty claim
Warranty claims require the order number. Repairs are completed within **10 business days** of the item arriving at our service centre. If a repair is not possible, we replace the item or issue a refund.
