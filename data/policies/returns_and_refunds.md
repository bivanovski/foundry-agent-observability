# VoltCart Returns and Refunds Policy

Effective 1 September 2026. Applies to all orders placed on voltcart.example.

## Return window
You can return most items within **30 calendar days of delivery**. The 30 days start on the delivery date shown in your order history, not the order date. Returns requested after 30 days are not accepted.

## Holiday extension
Items delivered between **15 November and 24 December** can be returned until **15 January** of the following year.

## Condition of returned items
Items must be complete and returned with all accessories and the original packaging.

## Items that cannot be returned
- **In-ear headphones and earbuds** cannot be returned once the hygiene seal has been broken, unless the item is defective.
- **Gift cards** cannot be returned or refunded.
- **Software licences and digital downloads** cannot be returned once the activation code has been revealed.

## Restocking fee
Opened **laptops and tablets** returned in working condition are subject to a **15% restocking fee**. Unopened laptops and tablets have no fee. No other product category has a restocking fee.

## Return shipping
- If the item is defective or we sent the wrong item, return shipping is free.
- For all other returns (for example, you changed your mind), the customer pays a flat return shipping fee of **4.99 EUR**, deducted from the refund.

## Refunds
Refunds are issued to the original payment method within **14 days** of the item arriving at our warehouse.

## How to start a return
Returns are started from the order page or by our support team using the order number.
