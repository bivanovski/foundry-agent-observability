# VoltCart Shipping and Pricing Policy

## Delivery options
- **Standard delivery:** 3 to 5 business days. Free on orders over **50 EUR**, otherwise 3.99 EUR.
- **Express delivery:** 1 to 2 business days for **9.99 EUR**.

## Where we ship
We ship to North Macedonia, Serbia, Bulgaria, Greece and Albania.

## Price match
If the price of an item on voltcart.example drops within **7 days of delivery**, we refund the difference on request. Price match applies only to prices on our own store, not to other retailers.
